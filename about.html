About Kwa Olga

More than just a car wash. Kwa Olga is a place to wash, chill, eat, drink and enjoy good vibes.

Who We Are

Kwa Olga Car Wash & Lifestyle Lounge combines professional vehicle care with food, drinks, entertainment and a welcoming social environment.

Our goal is to give customers a comfortable place to enjoy themselves while their vehicles receive quality care.

Our Mission

To provide quality car care while creating an enjoyable lifestyle experience for our customers.

Our Vision

To create a recognised car wash and lifestyle destination where customers can enjoy quality service, food, drinks and entertainment.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Customer Service
  • Professionalism
  • Community
  • Good Experiences
